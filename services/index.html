Capabilities

Services

We can be engaged for any scope — full architectural and construction services, or a single part of the process: design only, drawings only, documentation only, or on-site supervision only.

01 Architectural Design
+

Complete architectural services from initial concept through to construction documentation — for residential, commercial, institutional, healthcare, religious, and hospitality buildings.

What we deliver:

  • Concept & schematic design drawings
  • Detailed working drawings for construction
  • Tender drawings and bid documentation
  • 3D rendering & photorealistic visualisation
  • Urdu-language construction drawings — a unique MKA service. We produce drawings labelled in Urdu so that site labourers and local contractors can read them directly, reducing errors on site. We have done this consistently since the 2005 earthquake rehabilitation work, and very few firms in Pakistan offer this.

02 Construction Management
+

Full-time site supervision from foundation to handover — the most important service we offer and the one clients most frequently engage us for independently of design.

  • Full-time supervision from excavation to handover
  • Programme management & progress reporting
  • Quality control at every stage
  • Sub-contractor coordination
  • Economical material procurement — sourcing standard-quality materials at the best available price
  • Contract administration — tender documents, bills of quantities, bid evaluation, and final account settlement
View related projects → Send an enquiry →
03 Structural Engineering
+

Structural analysis, design, and detailing for all major construction systems. Coordinated with the architectural design from the outset, not added on later.

  • Reinforced concrete — flat slab, beam-slab, waffle, folded plates, shells
  • Structural steel — industrial factories, warehouse sheds, long-span structures
  • Masonry construction — load-bearing brick and stone; a system rarely engineered properly in Pakistan and a MKA speciality
  • Underground and overhead water reservoirs
  • Bridges, flyovers, and infrastructure structures
View industrial projects → Send an enquiry →
04 Town Planning
+

Master planning and urban design for residential schemes, mixed-use developments, and institutional campuses — from neighbourhood scale to developments exceeding 2,000 acres. Our Kallar Kahar project (2011–2012) covered 1,500 acres of landscaping and infrastructure under direct Prime Minister's supervision.

  • Site analysis and masterplanning
  • Road networks and infrastructure layout
  • Zoning, land use planning, and phasing
  • Landscaping within large-scale planning projects
View planning projects → Send an enquiry →
05 Interior Design
+

Interior planning, material specification, ceiling design, and furniture layout for commercial, institutional, and residential projects across Pakistan. Our interiors are designed as an integral part of the architecture — not as a separate exercise.

  • Space planning and interior layout
  • Ceiling design and lighting coordination
  • Material, finish, and fixture specification
  • Furniture and joinery design
View related projects → Send an enquiry →
06 Landscape Design
+

Landscape architecture for public parks, hotel grounds, residential gardens, and institutional campuses. Planting schemes, hard landscape, water features, and outdoor lighting — all designed for the local climate and maintainability.

  • Public parks and recreational facilities
  • Hotel and resort grounds
  • Residential garden design
  • Water features, fountains, and landscape infrastructure
View landscape projects → Send an enquiry →
07 Computer-Aided Design (CAD)
+

All drawings are produced digitally in AutoCAD. We also offer standalone CAD drafting services — clients who already have a design can engage us to produce professional, fully-dimensioned construction drawings from sketches or concepts.

  • AutoCAD construction and working drawings
  • 3D modelling and rendered visualisations
  • Series drawings for documentation packages
  • Urdu-annotated drawings (see Architectural Design)

Process

How We Engage

We offer each of these services independently — or as a complete end-to-end process. You do not have to start from the beginning. If you only need documentation, or only need supervision, you can engage us for exactly that, and nothing more.

Available standalone
01
Consultation
We meet, study the site, and help define the brief, budget, and programme. Useful even if you plan to engage another firm for design — an independent second opinion.
Available standalone
02
Design
Concept through to detailed design, with 3D renderings where useful. We can design a project even if you intend to manage construction separately.
Available standalone
03
Documentation
Construction drawings, structural details, AutoCAD drafting, and tender documents — produced from your existing design or concept. Urdu-annotated drawings available on request.
Available standalone
04
Supervision & Construction
Full-time site presence from foundation to handover. Quality control, material procurement, and cost monitoring. Engage us even if MKA did not produce the drawings.

You can start at any stage.  If your project is already designed and you need drawings — come to us at step 03. If your drawings are ready and you need someone on site — come to us at step 04. We will fit in wherever you need us.
