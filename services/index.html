Capabilities

Services

01 Architectural Design
+

Complete architectural services from initial concept through to construction documentation. Residential, commercial, institutional, healthcare, religious, and hospitality buildings. Every project is designed, detailed, and documented in-house.

02 Structural Engineering
+

Structural analysis, design, and detailing for reinforced concrete, steel, and masonry construction. Coordinated with the architectural design from the outset, not bolted on later.

03 Town Planning
+

Master planning and urban design for residential schemes, mixed-use developments, and institutional campuses. Site analysis, infrastructure layout, road networks, and zoning — from neighbourhood scale to developments exceeding 2,000 acres.

04 Interior Design
+

Interior planning, material specification, and furniture layout for commercial and residential projects. The interiors are designed as part of the architecture, not as a separate exercise.

05 Landscape Design
+

Landscape architecture for public parks, hotel grounds, residential gardens, and institutional campuses. Planting, hardscape, water features, and outdoor lighting — designed for the local climate.

06 Construction Management
+

Full-time site supervision from foundation to handover. Programme management, progress reporting, quality control, and coordination of all trades. We stay on site until the building is complete.

07 Contract Administration
+

Preparation of tender documents, bills of quantities, and contract conditions. Bid evaluation, contractor selection, and ongoing contract administration through to final account settlement.

08 Computer-Aided Design
+

Production of all drawings in AutoCAD and Revit. 3D modelling and photorealistic visualisation for client presentations and planning submissions.
