Our Story

Founded in 1998, Archform has grown to become one of Dubai’s largest and most distinguished architectural studios. Over the years, we have delivered iconic, award-winning buildings, urban designs, and interior spaces — not only across the UAE but also throughout the Middle East, Africa, and beyond. With headquarters in Dubai and a presence in key global cities, we proudly partner with clients worldwide.
