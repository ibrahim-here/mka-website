About

• Est. 1985, Lahore

Forty Years
of Practice

Majed Khan & Associates was established in 1985 in Lahore as a full-service practice of consulting architects, engineers, and town planners.

The work has always been broad. Over four decades, MKA has completed more than five hundred projects across Pakistan and internationally — including for the National Highway Authority, Defence Housing Authority, the Hashoo Group (PC Hotels), Lahore American School, and Sialkot Dry Port Trust.

The firm draws, details, and supervises its own work — keeping design intent intact from first sketch to final handover.
