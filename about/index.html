About

The Practice

Forty Years of Practice

Majed Khan & Associates was established in 1985 in Lahore as a practice of consulting architects, engineers, and town planners. Over four decades, the firm has completed more than five hundred projects across Pakistan and internationally — private residences, mosques, hospitals, hotels, shopping centres, industrial facilities, and master plans up to 2,000 acres.

The work has always been broad. MKA has designed for the National Highway Authority, Defence Housing Authority, the Hashoo Group, Lahore American School, Sialkot Dry Port Trust, and dozens of private clients who return because the buildings perform as promised.

The practice is led by Abdul Majed Khan and Muhammad Akbar from the Lahore studio at 98-E Model Town. Both are long-standing members of PCATP and PEC. The firm draws, details, and supervises its own work — a full-service model that keeps design intent intact from first sketch to final inspection.
