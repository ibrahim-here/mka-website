● MESSAGE FROM CEO

Four Decades
of Practice

Every project begins with listening. Every building begins with a plan. What endures is the work itself.

MKA was founded in 1986 as a practice of consulting architects, engineers, and town planners. Over forty years we have completed more than five hundred projects — from private residences to 2,000-acre master plans — across Pakistan and internationally.

Our work is straightforward: understand the brief, respect the site, design buildings that perform well and age honestly. We have no interest in architecture that exists only in renders. Everything we draw, we build.

— Abdul Majed Khan, Principal
