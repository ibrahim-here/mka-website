Iqbal Manzil

A residential project that marries traditional Pakistani living habits with modern architectural forms. The house features a structured brick-and-plaster facade with deep recesses, balcony overhangs, and elegant detailing that responds to the local climate. The plan separates public entertainment areas on the lower levels from private family quarters, utilizing high ceilings and large windows to ensure natural light and ventilation throughout.
